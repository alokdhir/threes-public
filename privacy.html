Privacy Policy for Threes

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Threes ("the app") is a pass-and-play dice game. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data collection

Threes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no account system.

Contacts access

Threes may request access to your Contacts to suggest player names when you set up a game. This is entirely optional — you can type player names by hand instead. Contact data is used only on your device to populate name suggestions and is never transmitted, stored outside your device, or shared with anyone, including us.

Game data

Player names and scores exist only in memory while the app is open and are discarded when the app is closed. Nothing is saved to a server or account.

Children's privacy

Threes does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the support link on the app's App Store page.
